Standard Thermal Formula
1 Calculate Heat Input to Engine
kJ/h
Output Power at Generator/Unit: 4090 kW/Unit
Total Gross Output (4090 × 3): 12270 kW

Rumus: Heat Input = Net Plant Heat Rate × Net Plant Power Output

Heat Input = 8086 kJ/kWh × 11064 kW

= 89,463,504 kJ/h

2 Calculate Fuel Gas Rate per Engine
MMSCFD
LCV (Lower Calorific Value): 49797.3 kJ/Nm³
Engine Unit (n): 3 Unit

Rumus: Fuel Gas Rate / Engine = Heat Input / (LCV × n)

= 89,463,504 / (49797.3 × 3)

= 89,463,504 / 149391.9

= 598.85 Nm³/h

= 0.536 MMSCFD

3 Result Summary
Fuel Gas Rate per Engine: 0.536 MMSCFD
Total Fuel Gas Rate (n × Engine): 1.608 MMSCFD

Total Fuel Gas Rate didapatkan dari perkalian jumlah unit engine (n) dengan laju konsumsi per engine.

4 Efisiensi Termal Bersih (Net Plant Efficiency)

Rumus: η_net = (3,600 / Net Plant Heat Rate) × 100%

η_net = (3600 / 8086) × 100

= 44.52 %

Konstanta 3,600 kJ mewakili energi ekivalen 1 kWh listrik.

Referensi Teknis & Definisi Satuan

MMSCFD

Million Standard Cubic Feet per Day (Juta Kaki Kubik Standar per Hari). Satuan standar industri minyak dan gas.

LCV (Lower Calorific Value)

Nilai Kalor Bawah gas alam yang memperhitungkan energi kalor tanpa kondensasi uap air hasil pembakaran.
